How I work

Measurements someone can rely on

The same habits run through my placements and my university projects.

Design the test

Write the protocol and the acceptance criteria first, from the product requirement or the relevant ISO, EN or ASTM standard.

Read the data critically

Separate what the material is doing from what the set-up is doing: grip seating, alignment, mesh density or boundary assumptions.

Check before anyone relies on it

Keep raw data untouched, date every change to the plan, and reproduce key results independently before reporting them.
